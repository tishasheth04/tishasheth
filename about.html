A Little About Me

Hello! I'm Tisha Sheth, an Honours Computer Science student at the University of Guelph. With a strong interest in coding and a drive for problem-solving, I have maintained an 86% average across my semesters while balancing academic excellence with hands-on technical experience.

For me, coding is more than just coursework it's a creative outlet and a space to explore new frameworks, technologies, and approaches. I'm especially drawn to building practical, engaging solutions and enjoy the process of turning ideas into functional applications.

One of my most rewarding academic experiences was in my Software Systems Development and Integration course, where I developed a full-stack virtual pool game. I engineered the game's physics engine to simulate realistic interactions, combining technical accuracy with user-centered design.

Outside the classroom, I have held various co-op roles in IT support and full-stack development. Each role has helped me build a broader understanding of the tech landscape while strengthening my communication and problem-solving skills in real-world settings.

When I’m not coding, I enjoy playing the piano, exploring new places, and volunteering in my community. Whether I’m supporting a cause I care about or learning something new, I’m always looking for meaningful ways to grow both personally and professionally.
